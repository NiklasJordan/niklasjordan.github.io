About me

I’m a Geologist working on safer communities. I help municipalities, planners and asset owners understand what natural hazards and a changing climate mean for them, and what to do about it.

Floods, storms, heat and other hazards look different everywhere, so I combine what I see on site with satellite data, geodata and modelling to build a clear picture of the risk. Then I turn it into information people can act on, because a risk nobody understands is a risk nobody acts on.

I’m based in Sønderborg Kommune, Denmark, where I founded geofare.

  • BSc (Hons) Geology, The Open University. Dissertation on storm-surge flood risk under global warming in Sønderborg kommune, Denmark.1
  • 15+ years connecting data and people. I lead a user support team for EUMETSAT’s satellite data services and have worked as a freelance Earth observation consultant. Started OpenSpaceData2 and DisasterTech3.
  • Qualified Stormwater Manager (QSM) by EnviroCert4, plus certifications in Urban Stormwater Management
  • Member of EnviroCert’s Foundational Certification Committee, overseeing the QSM, CIHECP and CPIESC certifications
  • Expert Panel member, Design for Emergency Management5
  • Co-author of the Greenbook Satellites and Sustainability6
  • Volunteer in the Danish emergency service
